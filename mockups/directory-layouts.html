HCH Member Directory — Layout Concepts

Rows for the list, faster paths to the full profile

Three ways to lay out each member as a row instead of a card, and three ways to surface everything about one member once you click in. All six use real fields from the response sheet — skills, project status, what someone's offering or looking for, and their contact opt-ins — so you're comparing how the data actually reads, not placeholder text.

Part 1 — Ways to list members

Same six members, three different bets on what matters most at a glance: raw scannability, what someone needs or offers, or getting straight to contacting them.

Part 2 — Ways to show one full profile

Same member (Priya Shah) in all three, so the layouts are the only variable. Each one starts open — collapse it and reopen to feel how many clicks it actually costs.

1

Modal overlay

Best for: one focused read. Dims everything else until you dismiss it.
2

Slide-over drawer

Best for: going profile to profile — the list stays put behind it.
3

Inline expand

Best for: zero navigation — nothing opens on top of the list, it just grows.

If you want one recommendation: B + 3 — the two-line context row paired with inline expand — gets you closest to "see it without clicking" for both the list and the detail. A + 1 reads cleaner if the roster gets large and occasional full-modal dives are fine. C's action rail is worth keeping regardless of which detail style wins — letting people email or open a link straight from the row is the actual fewest-clicks move, detail view or not.
